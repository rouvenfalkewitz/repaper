01 / IDENTITY

Identity & logo

RePaper for the shop floor. Built like the equipment it replaces: unfussy, high-visibility, readable from three metres in bad light with gloves on.

Name

RePaper — one word. In this direction the wordmark is set in caps, extra-wide Archivo, like a stencil on a crate. "RE" in Safety Orange, "PAPER" in Steel. repaper in code.

Logo mark

Direction for the mark: a label with a tear line and a return arrow — a rectangle with one perforated edge, the arrow looping back through it. Blocky, stencil-cut, no curves finer than the stroke. Must work as a stencil, a die-cut sticker and an LED silhouette.

LOGO MARK — PENDING
Square slot. Stencil-safe: no islands, no thin bridges.
  • Caps wordmark, wide width (125%), tracking −2%
  • Mark and wordmark share a baseline; mark height = cap height
  • Clear space = height of one letter
  • Mono in Steel or Chalk only
  • No rounded, lower-case or narrow versions of the wordmark
  • No gradients, no glow, no metallic
  • Never orange text on steel for body copy
  • Don't use the orange for anything but "act now"

The rule is the one from safety signage: black text on the colour, never the colour as text on black — except for links. Orange means "act now" (a job is waiting); green means OK; yellow means decide; red means stop. Nothing else may use these.

Dark mode

Ground Steel-dark #0F141C, surfaces #171E29, frames become Chalk. The Dock's own UI is dark by default, like a machine HMI.

On the sheet

Archivo Condensed 700 for headers (it fits long location codes), Source Sans 600 for body, JetBrains Mono for codes. Minimum 12 px body / 9 px mono. Every sheet gets a barcode or code strip at the bottom by default — a sheet that can be scanned is a sheet that fits the warehouse.

04 / LAYOUT

Layout

Square corners, 2 px frames, dashed tear lines between sections. Surfaces are white labels on a chalk ground. No shadows — a label lies flat.

Nothing is rounder than 4 px. Borders are 2 px Steel for anything you can act on, 1 px Steel 200 for anything you only read.

Structure

Sections are separated by a dashed tear line (2px dashed), not whitespace alone. Numbered eyebrows (02 / COLOR) because everything in a warehouse is numbered. Left-aligned, never centred. Reading width 64 characters, content max 1180 px.

07 / VOICE

Voice

Terse, operational, zero adjectives. Written like the best equipment manuals: what it is, what to do, what happened.

  • Imperatives: "Hold a sheet." "Take the sheet."
  • State + instruction, always in that order.
  • Numbers over words: "2,140 sheets saved", "Route 3", "00:12 ago".
  • Caps for headings and states; sentence case for everything else.
  • No warmth padding ("Great, all done!").
  • No IoT vocabulary: gateway, sync, provision, firmware, tag.
  • No "smart", no "eco".
  • No sentence longer than one line on a 4.2" sheet.
