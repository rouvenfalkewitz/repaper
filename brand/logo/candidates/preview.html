Notes

  • Arrows didn't survive. Two attempts to put a return arrow into the pixels (as solid cells, and as a cut-out) both collapsed into a plus-sign / plug shape at this level of simplicity. The arrow needs curves and fine proportions that a coarse pixel grid can't give it — and a smooth arrow next to pixels looked like two different logos. If "return" must be in the mark, A3 says it with a re-forming sheet instead.
  • A1 vs A2 is a tone decision: A1 is harder and more "1-bit"; A2 is friendlier and echoes the LED / rounded-corner language. Both hold at 16 px as "a square with a dissolving edge".
  • A4 (RE) is the combined symbol. It's strong on its own (app icon, sticker, diffuser) but next to the wordmark it repeats "RE" — usable if the lockup uses the mark or the wordmark, not both, or if the mark stands alone on hardware.
  • None of the dissolves is stencil-safe (loose pixels); for emboss/diffuser the silhouette (full rounded square, or the RE outline) is used instead — that's normal for pixel-based marks.
