Notes

  • Geometry: sheet 520 × 640 (4:5), corner radius 64 (12 %), fold 190 (35 % of width), stroke 80 (15 %), arrow hook radius 90, arrowhead 120 long.
  • The fold's inner edge is the arrow's tail — the "page comes back" idea is literal, not decorative.
  • 01 and 01b share one geometry, so they can coexist: 01 for UI and lockups, 01b for icons, stickers and 1-bit on-sheet use. Pick one as primary.
  • Known risk: it is close to generic "import / reply" document icons. The wide-caps wordmark and the two-tone RE/PAPER do a lot of the distinguishing — decide if that's enough or if the mark needs more of its own idea.
