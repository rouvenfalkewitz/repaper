Notes

  • RE in the dissolve only became legible once the noise was removed and the R got a fourth column: pixel letters need clean space around them. A6 is the readable one; A6s is the same in LED-style pixels (it looks like a small e-paper/LED matrix, which is on-brand); A7 lets the dissolve continue through the E, which costs legibility. At 16 px all three become "a square with a ragged edge" — fine for a favicon, and the RE is a large-size reward.
  • RE duplication still applies: next to the REPAPER wordmark the lockup says "RE REPAPER". If A6 wins, the mark stands alone on hardware/icons and the wordmark stands alone in lockups — or the lockup keeps only the solid sheet + a few pixels and the RE version is the "full" mark.
  • Digital tree (T4) — a round pixel canopy on a trunk, leaves blowing off as pixels to the top-right. It is charming and it's the only one that says "green" — but it also reads as a lollipop/dandelion at small sizes and edges toward the "eco" imagery the brand rules ban. If you want it, it should probably be a secondary illustration (website, sustainability page, merch) rather than the mark.
  • Dropped this round: a pixel pine (read as a Space Invader), a square canopy (ice cream), the dissolve on a stick (lollipop), and the noisy RE versions (illegible R).
