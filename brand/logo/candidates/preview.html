Notes

  • T1 · Canopy is the friendliest and the closest to the LED/rounded language. It's also the most "tree" and therefore the most at risk of reading as eco-generic; the pixels are what keep it ours.
  • T2 · 8-bit is the most honest to e-ink: everything is a pixel, including the trunk. It holds best at 16 px and in 1-bit, and it's the easiest to animate (the crown blowing away, pixel by pixel, on the website hero or the Dock's boot screen).
  • T3 · Circuit is the most "futuristic/digital" and the most ownable — nobody else's tree looks like a trace with pixel leaves — but it is also the least instantly "tree"; people may need the word next to it once.
  • Brand-rule note: the guide currently bans "eco imagery — leaves, plants, earth". If the tree becomes the mark, that rule gets rewritten to "the tree is ours, drawn only in pixels; never a photographic or illustrated leaf".
  • None is stencil-safe (loose pixels). For emboss/diffuser use the silhouette without the drifting pixels.
