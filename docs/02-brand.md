# 02 · Brand

The full brand system lives in [`../brand/`](../brand/README.md) — open `brand/guidelines.html` in a browser for the visual guide, and use `brand/tokens.css` / `brand/tokens.json` in any UI. This page keeps only the strategic bits.

## Name

**RePaper** — "re" as in reusable, reprint, rethink. Paper that comes back.
Spelling: `RePaper` in prose and logo; `repaper` in code, domains, handles.

## Tagline

*Reusable paper. Printed the way you already print.* (current)
Alternatives kept on file: *Print it again.* · *The last sheet you'll ever need.* · *Paper, reinvented. Printing, untouched.*

## Positioning statement

For organisations that run on printed labels, lists and signs, RePaper is a plug-and-play printer replacement that prints onto reusable e-paper instead of paper — cutting waste and cost without changing a single workflow.

## Product naming (working names — to be decided)

Sheet sizes use paper analogies in customer-facing copy: *Sheet Label* (2.9"), *Sheet Card* (4.2"), *Sheet Page* (7.5"), *Sheet Letter* (13.3") — final mapping depends on the panels we source.

## Customer-facing story in three sentences

1. Your systems keep printing exactly as they do today.
2. Instead of a printer, they print to RePaper — and the page appears on a reusable e-paper sheet.
3. Reuse the sheet thousands of times. No paper, no toner, no integration project.
